About Me

I'm a proud alumna of University at Buffalo (SUNY) and Texas Southern University. My background in human factors and systems thinking continues to shape how I approach digital experiences, with empathy, usability, and real-world impact in mind.

I specialize in building fast, inclusive websites that work beautifully across devices. My approach combines accessible code, thoughtful UX, and data-driven design decisions to ensure each project is both user-friendly and high performing. Whether I'm developing from scratch or working within a CMS like Shopify, I focus on creating experiences that are intuitive, responsive, and built with purpose.

In my free time, I enjoy traveling, spending time with family, exploring different cuisines, and having fun with photography.
